Accommodation

The conference hotel is the Grand Hyatt Seattle where conference rates have been negotiated to be:

  • $185 for single/double king bed
  • $200 for single/double two beds
  • $210 for triple
  • $235 for quadruple

All rooms include complimentary internet. The Group rate will be available up to three days pre/post conference based upon the availability of the Hotel. Attendees can access the site to learn more about the event and to book, modify, or cancel a reservation from August 23, 2013 to September 27, 2013.

To make your hotel reservations and take advantage of the Conference Group Rate, please follow this link or use the passcode EMNLP2013:
